## What I build

**[Sunday Reset](https://github.com/donut-codes/sunday-reset)**: a weekly life planner that runs as a Claude Code plugin.

- Before: planning a week meant checking a calendar, an inbox, a grocery site and a to-do app separately.
- After: one email each week with workouts placed around the calendar, dinners, a grocery list with live prices, and bills and free trials pulled from the inbox. Items are added to the calendar and to-do list.
- It runs unattended on a schedule, limited to an explicit list of allowed actions. Try the setup page at [sundayreset.cameroncrump.com](https://sundayreset.cameroncrump.com/).

**[resumebot (fork)](https://github.com/donut-codes/resumebot/tree/mac-compat-on-2.9.2)**: an open-source job-search app by Eric Wurth, where agents find and score roles and the person applies.

- My fork ported the app to the Mac when it was Windows-only (branch `mac-compat`).
- I added a per-employer limit so one or two companies can't fill the whole shortlist.
